FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Find answers to common questions about professional anaesthesia care, patient safety and healthcare services.

What does a Registered Nurse Anaesthetist do?

A Registered Nurse Anaesthetist provides safe anaesthesia care, monitors patients during procedures and supports recovery before, during and after surgery.

Why is patient safety important during anaesthesia?

Patient safety is the highest priority. Careful assessment, monitoring and professional clinical judgement help reduce risks and improve surgical outcomes.

Do you work with other healthcare professionals?

Yes. Anaesthesia care involves close collaboration with surgeons, physicians, nurses and other healthcare professionals to ensure coordinated patient care.

Are you committed to continuous professional development?

Yes. Continuous learning ensures healthcare professionals remain updated with current clinical guidelines and best practices.
